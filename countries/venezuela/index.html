Hummingbirds of Venezuela

104 of the world's 363 hummingbird species live in or visit Venezuela, among them the Green-tailed Emerald and the Merida Sunangel. 6 of them are found nowhere else on Earth. Only Colombia, Brazil, Mexico and Peru have more species of their own.
