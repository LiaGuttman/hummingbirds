Hummingbirds of Peru

130 of the world's 363 hummingbird species live in or visit Peru, among them the Green-and-white Hummingbird and the Black Metaltail. 13 of them are found nowhere else on Earth. Only Colombia, Brazil and Mexico have more species of their own.
