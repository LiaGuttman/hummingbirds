Polytminae

Subfamily · mangoes · family Trochilidae

Polytminae, the mangoes, is a subfamily of hummingbirds with 30 species in 12 genera. They are found in 47 countries; the most live in Colombia (16), Venezuela (15) and Brazil (14). 4 are found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
