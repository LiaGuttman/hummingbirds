Chlorostilbon

Genus · emeralds · family Trochilidae

Chlorostilbon is a genus of 10 species of hummingbirds (the emeralds) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 19 countries; the most live in Colombia (7), Venezuela (6) and Bolivia (2). 2 are found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
