Phaethornithinae

Subfamily · hermits · family Trochilidae

Phaethornithinae, the hermits, is a subfamily of hummingbirds with 37 species in 6 genera. They are found in 20 countries; the most live in Brazil (22), Colombia (20) and Ecuador (17). 8 are found in only one country. 2 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
