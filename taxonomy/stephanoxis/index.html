Stephanoxis

Genus · plovercrests · family Trochilidae

Stephanoxis is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the plovercrests) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 3 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
