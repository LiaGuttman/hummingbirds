Saucerottia

Genus · family Trochilidae

Saucerottia is a genus of 11 species of hummingbirds in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 15 countries; the most live in Colombia (5), Venezuela (4) and Guatemala (3). 2 are found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
