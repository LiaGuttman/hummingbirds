Lesbia

Genus · trainbearers · family Trochilidae

Lesbia is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the trainbearers) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 4 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Lesbia share: Extremely long, deeply forked tails.
