Amazilia

Genus · emeralds · family Trochilidae

Amazilia is a genus of 4 species of hummingbirds (the emeralds) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 13 countries. 1 is found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
