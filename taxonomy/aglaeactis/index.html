Aglaeactis

Genus · sunbeams · family Trochilidae

Aglaeactis is a genus of 4 species of hummingbirds (the sunbeams) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 4 countries. 3 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Aglaeactis share: Males have a patch of glittering color on the back; in females it is duller or missing.
