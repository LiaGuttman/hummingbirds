Urochroa

Genus · hillstars · family Trochilidae

Urochroa is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the hillstars) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 3 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
