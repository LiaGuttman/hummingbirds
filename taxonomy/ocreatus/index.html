Ocreatus

Genus · racket-tails · family Trochilidae

Ocreatus is a genus of 3 species of hummingbirds (the racket-tails) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Ocreatus share: Males' outer tail feathers end in rackets, and the legs have puffy feathers ('boots').
