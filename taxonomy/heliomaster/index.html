Heliomaster

Genus · starthroats · family Trochilidae

Heliomaster is a genus of 4 species of hummingbirds (the starthroats) in the tribe Lampornithini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 21 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Heliomaster share: Very long, straight bills.
