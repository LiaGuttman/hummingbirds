Thalurania

Genus · woodnymphs · family Trochilidae

Thalurania is a genus of 4 species of hummingbirds (the woodnymphs) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 17 countries. 1 is found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
