Heliangelus

Genus · sunangels · family Trochilidae

Heliangelus is a genus of 9 species of hummingbirds (the sunangels) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries; the most live in Ecuador (6), Colombia (4) and Peru (4). 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Heliangelus share: Most males have a glittering throat patch (gorget).
