Glaucis

Genus · hermits · family Trochilidae

Glaucis is a genus of 3 species of hummingbirds (the hermits) in the subfamily Phaethornithinae. They are found in 15 countries. 1 is found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
