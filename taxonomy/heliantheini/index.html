Heliantheini

Tribe · brilliants · family Trochilidae

Heliantheini, the brilliants, is a tribe of hummingbirds with 57 species in 13 genera, part of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 11 countries; the most live in Colombia (42), Ecuador (34) and Peru (30). 13 are found in only one country. 8 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
