Aglaiocercus

Genus · sylphs · family Trochilidae

Aglaiocercus is a genus of 3 species of hummingbirds (the sylphs) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries. 1 is found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Aglaiocercus share: Males have very long, iridescent tails.
