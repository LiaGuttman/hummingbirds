Selasphorus

Genus · family Trochilidae

Selasphorus is a genus of 9 species of hummingbirds in the tribe Mellisugini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 11 countries; the most live in Mexico (6), Canada (4) and the United States (4). 2 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Selasphorus share: Includes some of the longest-migrating hummingbirds, such as the Rufous Hummingbird.
