Trochilinae

Subfamily · mountain gems, bees and emeralds · family Trochilidae

Trochilinae, the mountain gems, bees and emeralds, is a subfamily of hummingbirds with 167 species in 60 genera. They are found in 53 countries; the most live in Colombia (55), Mexico (50) and Ecuador (38). 40 are found in only one country. 14 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
