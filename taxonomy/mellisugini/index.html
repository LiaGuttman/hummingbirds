Mellisugini

Tribe · bees · family Trochilidae

Mellisugini, the bees, is a tribe of hummingbirds with 37 species in 17 genera, part of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 33 countries; the most live in Mexico (15), the United States (10) and Ecuador (9). 9 are found in only one country. 3 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
