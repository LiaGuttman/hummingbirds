Discosura

Genus · thorntails · family Trochilidae

Discosura is a genus of 5 species of hummingbirds (the thorntails) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 11 countries; the most live in Colombia (4), Bolivia (3) and Ecuador (3). 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Discosura share: Males have thin, spiky tail feathers.
