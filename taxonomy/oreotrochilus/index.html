Oreotrochilus

Genus · hillstars · family Trochilidae

Oreotrochilus is a genus of 7 species of hummingbirds (the hillstars) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 6 countries; the most live in Ecuador (3), Peru (3) and Argentina (3). 2 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Oreotrochilus share: Live high in the Andes, up to the snow line, and can drop into torpor on cold nights.
