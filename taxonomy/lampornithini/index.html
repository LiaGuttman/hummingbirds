Lampornithini

Tribe · mountain gems · family Trochilidae

Lampornithini, the mountain gems, is a tribe of hummingbirds with 17 species in 7 genera, part of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 22 countries; the most live in Honduras (7), Mexico (7) and Costa Rica (7). 2 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
