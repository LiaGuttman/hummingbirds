Microchera

Genus · snowcaps, emeralds · family Trochilidae

Microchera is a genus of 3 species of hummingbirds (the snowcaps, emeralds) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 4 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
