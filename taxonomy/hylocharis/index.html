Hylocharis

Genus · sapphires · family Trochilidae

Hylocharis is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the sapphires) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 12 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
