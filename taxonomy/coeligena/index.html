Coeligena

Genus · incas, starfrontlets · family Trochilidae

Coeligena is a genus of 15 species of hummingbirds (the incas, starfrontlets) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries; the most live in Colombia (11), Peru (6) and Venezuela (6). 5 are found in only one country. 3 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Coeligena share: Long, straight bills.
