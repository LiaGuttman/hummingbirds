Eriocnemis

Genus · pufflegs · family Trochilidae

Eriocnemis is a genus of 11 species of hummingbirds (the pufflegs) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 6 countries; the most live in Colombia (9), Ecuador (7) and Peru (3). 3 are found in only one country. 4 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Eriocnemis share: Fluffy 'puffs' of feathers on the legs, often white.
