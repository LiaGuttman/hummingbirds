Metallura

Genus · metaltails · family Trochilidae

Metallura is a genus of 9 species of hummingbirds (the metaltails) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries; the most live in Peru (6), Ecuador (4) and Colombia (3). 4 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Metallura share: Tails glossed with metallic color.
