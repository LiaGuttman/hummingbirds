Lesbiinae

Subfamily · coquettes and brilliants · family Trochilidae

Lesbiinae, the coquettes and brilliants, is a subfamily of hummingbirds with 124 species in 31 genera. They are found in 22 countries; the most live in Colombia (72), Ecuador (63) and Peru (63). 35 are found in only one country. 16 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
