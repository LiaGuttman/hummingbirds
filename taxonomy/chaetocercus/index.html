Chaetocercus

Genus · woodstars · family Trochilidae

Chaetocercus is a genus of 6 species of hummingbirds (the woodstars) in the tribe Mellisugini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 6 countries; the most live in Colombia (5), Ecuador (4) and Peru (2). 2 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Chaetocercus share: Among the smallest birds in the world.
