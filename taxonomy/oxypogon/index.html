Oxypogon

Genus · helmetcrests · family Trochilidae

Oxypogon is a genus of 4 species of hummingbirds (the helmetcrests) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 2 countries. 4 are found in only one country. 2 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Oxypogon share: Males have a magnificent spiky crest and a long beard. They live in high páramo grassland in Colombia and Venezuela.
