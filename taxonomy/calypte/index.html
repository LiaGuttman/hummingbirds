Calypte

Genus · family Trochilidae

Calypte is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds in the tribe Mellisugini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 3 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Calypte share: Males have a glittering crown, and their glittering throat feathers stretch out into points at the sides.
