Eulampis

Genus · caribs · family Trochilidae

Eulampis is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the caribs) in the subfamily Polytminae. They are found in 18 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Eulampis share: Caribs live on the islands of the Lesser Antilles and have down-curved bills.
