Urosticte

Genus · whitetips · family Trochilidae

Urosticte is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the whitetips) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 3 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
