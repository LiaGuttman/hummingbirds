Chalcostigma

Genus · thornbills · family Trochilidae

Chalcostigma is a genus of 5 species of hummingbirds (the thornbills) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries; the most live in Peru (4), Bolivia (3) and Ecuador (3). None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Chalcostigma share: Short bills.
