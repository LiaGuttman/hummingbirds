Heliodoxa

Genus · brilliants, jewelfronts, rubies · family Trochilidae

Heliodoxa is a genus of 10 species of hummingbirds (the brilliants, jewelfronts, rubies) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 10 countries; the most live in Colombia (7), Ecuador (7) and Peru (6). 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
