Eutoxeres

Genus · sicklebills · family Trochilidae

Eutoxeres is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the sicklebills) in the subfamily Phaethornithinae. They are found in 5 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Eutoxeres share: The bill curves sharply downward, matching the curved flowers of plants such as Heliconia.
