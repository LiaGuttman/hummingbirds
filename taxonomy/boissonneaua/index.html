Boissonneaua

Genus · coronets · family Trochilidae

Boissonneaua is a genus of 3 species of hummingbirds (the coronets) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 4 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Boissonneaua share: Often hold their wings raised for a moment after landing.
