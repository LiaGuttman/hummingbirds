Elliotomyia

Genus · family Trochilidae

Elliotomyia is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 4 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
