Lampornis

Genus · mountain-gems · family Trochilidae

Lampornis is a genus of 7 species of hummingbirds (the mountain-gems) in the tribe Lampornithini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 9 countries; the most live in Honduras (3), Mexico (3) and Costa Rica (3). None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Lampornis share: Live in highland forests from the southwestern United States to Panama.
