Ramphomicron

Genus · thornbills · family Trochilidae

Ramphomicron is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the thornbills) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Ramphomicron share: Very short bills.
