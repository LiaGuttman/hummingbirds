Haplophaedia

Genus · pufflegs · family Trochilidae

Haplophaedia is a genus of 3 species of hummingbirds (the pufflegs) in the tribe Heliantheini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 5 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Haplophaedia share: Fluffy 'puffs' of feathers on the legs.
