Phlogophilus

Genus · piedtails · family Trochilidae

Phlogophilus is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the piedtails) in the tribe Lesbiini of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 3 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
