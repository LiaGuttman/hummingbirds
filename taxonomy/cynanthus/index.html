Cynanthus

Genus · emeralds · family Trochilidae

Cynanthus is a genus of 6 species of hummingbirds (the emeralds) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 9 countries; the most live in Mexico (6), Canada (1) and the United States (1). 4 are found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
