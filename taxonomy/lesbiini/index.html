Lesbiini

Tribe · coquettes · family Trochilidae

Lesbiini, the coquettes, is a tribe of hummingbirds with 67 species in 18 genera, part of the subfamily Lesbiinae. They are found in 22 countries; the most live in Peru (33), Colombia (30) and Ecuador (29). 22 are found in only one country. 8 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
