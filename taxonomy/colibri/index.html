Colibri

Genus · violetears · family Trochilidae

Colibri is a genus of 5 species of hummingbirds (the violetears) in the subfamily Polytminae. They are found in 22 countries; the most live in Bolivia (4), Colombia (3) and Ecuador (3). None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Colibri share: A patch of violet-blue feathers behind the eye (the 'ear') that can be fanned out in display.
