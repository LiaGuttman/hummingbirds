Pampa

Genus · sabrewings · family Trochilidae

Pampa is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the sabrewings) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 5 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Pampa share: Males have thickened, bent shafts on their outer wing feathers (the 'sabres').
