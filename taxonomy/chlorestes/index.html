Chlorestes

Genus · emeralds, goldentails, sapphires · family Trochilidae

Chlorestes is a genus of 5 species of hummingbirds (the emeralds, goldentails, sapphires) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 18 countries; the most live in Colombia (4), Peru (3) and Costa Rica (2). None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
