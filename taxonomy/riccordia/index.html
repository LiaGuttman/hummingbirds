Riccordia

Genus · emeralds · family Trochilidae

Riccordia is a genus of 5 species of hummingbirds (the emeralds) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 8 countries; the most live in the Bahamas (2), Cuba (1) and the United States (1). 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
