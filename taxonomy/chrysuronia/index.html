Chrysuronia

Genus · emeralds, sapphires · family Trochilidae

Chrysuronia is a genus of 10 species of hummingbirds (the emeralds, sapphires) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 14 countries; the most live in Colombia (7), Venezuela (5) and Brazil (4). 2 are found in only one country. 2 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.
