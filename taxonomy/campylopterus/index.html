Campylopterus

Genus · sabrewings · family Trochilidae

Campylopterus is a genus of 10 species of hummingbirds (the sabrewings) in the tribe Trochilini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 18 countries; the most live in Brazil (5), Venezuela (5) and Colombia (4). 3 are found in only one country. 2 are threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Campylopterus share: Males have thickened, bent shafts on their outer wing feathers (the 'sabres').
