Nesophlox

Genus · woodstars · family Trochilidae

Nesophlox is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the woodstars) in the tribe Mellisugini of the subfamily Trochilinae. They are found in 3 countries. 1 is found in only one country. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.
