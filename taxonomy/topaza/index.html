Topaza

Genus · topazes · family Trochilidae

Topaza is a genus of 2 species of hummingbirds (the topazes) in the subfamily Florisuginae. They are found in 8 countries. None is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Topaza share: Males have two spectacular, very long central tail feathers that cross over each other.
