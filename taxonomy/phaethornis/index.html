Phaethornis

Genus · hermits · family Trochilidae

Phaethornis is a genus of 27 species of hummingbirds (the hermits) in the subfamily Phaethornithinae. They are found in 19 countries; the most live in Brazil (16), Colombia (14) and Peru (13). 5 are found in only one country. 1 is threatened on the IUCN Red List.

What Phaethornis share: Long, curved bills and long central tail feathers, often white-tipped. Males gather in leks to sing.
