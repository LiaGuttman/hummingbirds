Hummingbirds

      About & credits

      This explorer shows the 363 hummingbird species (family Trochilidae) recognized in the eBird/Clements taxonomy. Each shape is one species; photos will come later.

      Data sources

      Species list and names
      eBird/Clements Checklist of Birds of the World, via the eBird API (Cornell Lab of Ornithology). English and Spanish (Mexico) names come from the same source. IOC names, where they differ, are from the IOC World Bird List v15 (Gill, Donsker & Rasmussen, eds.).
      Subfamilies and tribes
      Dickinson, E.C. & Remsen, J.V. Jr. (eds.) 2013. The Howard and Moore Complete Checklist of the Birds of the World, 4th ed., vol. 1. Aves Press.
      McGuire, J.A. et al. 2014. Molecular phylogenetics and the diversification of hummingbirds. Current Biology 24: 910–916. The informal clade names (topazes, hermits, mangoes, coquettes, brilliants, Patagona, mountain gems, bees, emeralds) follow this paper.
      Countries
      BirdLife International DataZone country lists, checked against eBird records from the eBird Observation Dataset (Cornell Lab of Ornithology, CC BY 4.0), accessed through the GBIF.org API. Six rare species use ranges from the published literature.
      Weight, bill, habitat, movement
      AVONET: Tobias, J.A. et al. 2022. AVONET: morphological, ecological and geographical data for all birds. Ecology Letters 25: 581–597. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Recently split species use the values of the species they were split from.
      Conservation status and trend
      BirdLife International (the IUCN Red List authority for birds), and the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species via GBIF where a species doesn't match a BirdLife species one-to-one.
      Map
      Natural Earth (public domain), via the world-atlas package.

      Notes on groups

      The short "What they share" notes are being reviewed against published sources. The weight, bill, habitat, country and status figures beside them are calculated from the data above.
