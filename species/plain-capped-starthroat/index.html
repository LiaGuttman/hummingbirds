Plain-capped Starthroat

Heliomaster constantii (Delattre, 1843)

Spanish: Colibrí Picudo Occidental

The Plain-capped Starthroat (Heliomaster constantii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 7.3 g and its bill is 35.8 mm long. It lives in Mexico, Costa Rica, United States, Nicaragua, El Salvador, Honduras and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
