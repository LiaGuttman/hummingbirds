Green-headed Hillstar

Oreotrochilus stolzmanni Salvin, 1895

Spanish: Colibrí de Stolzmann

The Green-headed Hillstar (Oreotrochilus stolzmanni) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 8.7 g and its bill is 22.7 mm long. It lives in Peru and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
