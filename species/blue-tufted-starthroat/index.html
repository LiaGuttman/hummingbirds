Blue-tufted Starthroat

Heliomaster furcifer (Shaw, 1812)

Spanish: Colibrí de Barbijo

The Blue-tufted Starthroat (Heliomaster furcifer) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 5.4 g and its bill is 32.6 mm long. It lives in Argentina, Paraguay, Brazil, Uruguay and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
