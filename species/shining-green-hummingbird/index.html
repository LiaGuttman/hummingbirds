Shining-green Hummingbird

Chrysuronia goudoti (Bourcier, 1843)

Spanish: Colibrí de Goudot

The Shining-green Hummingbird (Chrysuronia goudoti) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.0 g and its bill is 19.6 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a increasing population.
