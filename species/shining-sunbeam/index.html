Shining Sunbeam

Aglaeactis cupripennis (Bourcier, 1843)

Spanish: Colibrí Cobrizo

The Shining Sunbeam (Aglaeactis cupripennis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 7.6 g and its bill is 22.5 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
