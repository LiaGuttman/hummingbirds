Plain-bellied Emerald

Chrysuronia leucogaster (Gmelin, JF, 1788)

Spanish: Amazilia Ventriblanca

The Plain-bellied Emerald (Chrysuronia leucogaster) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.7 g and its bill is 24.8 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Guyana, French Guiana, Suriname and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
