Berylline Hummingbird

Saucerottia beryllina (Deppe, 1830)

Spanish: Colibrí Berilo

The Berylline Hummingbird (Saucerottia beryllina) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 21.3 mm long. It lives in Mexico, United States, Guatemala, El Salvador and Honduras. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
