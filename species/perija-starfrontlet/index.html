Perija Starfrontlet

Coeligena consita Wetmore & Phelps, WH Jr, 1952

Spanish: Inca de Perijá

The Perija Starfrontlet (Coeligena consita) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.5 g and its bill is 37.8 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
