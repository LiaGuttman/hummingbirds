Purple-throated Carib

Eulampis jugularis (Linnaeus, 1766)

Spanish: Colibrí Caribeño Gorjimorado

The Purple-throated Carib (Eulampis jugularis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 8.9 g and its bill is 27.5 mm long. It lives in 9 countries, including Guadeloupe, Saint Lucia, Dominica, Martinique and St Vincent and the Grenadines. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
