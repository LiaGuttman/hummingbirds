Tawny-bellied Hermit

Phaethornis syrmatophorus Gould, 1852

Spanish: Ermitaño Ventrihabano

The Tawny-bellied Hermit (Phaethornis syrmatophorus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.8 g and its bill is 43.1 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
