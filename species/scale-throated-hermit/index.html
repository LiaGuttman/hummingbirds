Scale-throated Hermit

Phaethornis eurynome (Lesson, RP, 1832)

Spanish: Ermitaño Escamoso

The Scale-throated Hermit (Phaethornis eurynome) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.3 g and its bill is 39.6 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Argentina and Paraguay. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
