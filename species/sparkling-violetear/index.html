Sparkling Violetear

Colibri coruscans (Gould, 1846)

Spanish: Colibrí Rutilante

The Sparkling Violetear (Colibri coruscans) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 6.7 g and its bill is 29.1 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela, Bolivia, Argentina, Chile and Guyana. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
