Rufous-webbed Brilliant

Heliodoxa branickii (Taczanowski, 1874)

Spanish: Brillante Alicanela

The Rufous-webbed Brilliant (Heliodoxa branickii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 5.2 g and its bill is 25.6 mm long. It lives in Peru and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
