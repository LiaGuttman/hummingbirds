Wire-crested Thorntail

Discosura popelairii (Du Bus de Gisignies, 1846)

Spanish: Rabudito Crestado

The Wire-crested Thorntail (Discosura popelairii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 2.5 g and its bill is 15.7 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Peru, Colombia and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
