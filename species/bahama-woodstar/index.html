Bahama Woodstar

Nesophlox evelynae (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Colibrí de las Bahamas

The Bahama Woodstar (Nesophlox evelynae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.4 g and its bill is 17.7 mm long. It lives in the Bahamas, the Turks and Caicos Islands and the United States. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
