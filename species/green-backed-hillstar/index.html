Green-backed Hillstar

Urochroa leucura Lawrence, 1864

Spanish: Colibrí de Bouguer Oriental

The Green-backed Hillstar (Urochroa leucura) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 11.4 g and its bill is 32.2 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
