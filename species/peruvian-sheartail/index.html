Peruvian Sheartail

Thaumastura cora (Lesson, RP & Garnot, 1827)

Spanish: Colibrí Cora

The Peruvian Sheartail (Thaumastura cora) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 1.9 g and its bill is 16.0 mm long. It lives in Peru, Chile and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
