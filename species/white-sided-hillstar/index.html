White-sided Hillstar

Oreotrochilus leucopleurus Gould, 1847

Spanish: Colibrí Cordillerano

The White-sided Hillstar (Oreotrochilus leucopleurus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 8.2 g and its bill is 24.3 mm long. It lives in Chile, Argentina and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
