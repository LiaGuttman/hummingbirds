Western Emerald

Chlorostilbon melanorhynchus Gould, 1860

Spanish: Western Emerald

The Western Emerald (Chlorostilbon melanorhynchus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 2.6 g and its bill is 16.0 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
