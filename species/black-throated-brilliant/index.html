Black-throated Brilliant

Heliodoxa schreibersii (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Brillante Ventrinegro

The Black-throated Brilliant (Heliodoxa schreibersii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 9.2 g and its bill is 27.4 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia, Peru and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
