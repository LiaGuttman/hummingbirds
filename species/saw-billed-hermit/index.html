Saw-billed Hermit

Ramphodon naevius (Dumont, 1818)

Spanish: Ermitaño Picosierra

The Saw-billed Hermit (Ramphodon naevius) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 7.9 g and its bill is 39.4 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
