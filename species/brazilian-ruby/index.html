Brazilian Ruby

Heliodoxa rubricauda (Boddaert, 1783)

Spanish: Colibrí Colirrojo

The Brazilian Ruby (Heliodoxa rubricauda) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 7.9 g and its bill is 25.0 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
