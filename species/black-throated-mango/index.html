Black-throated Mango

Anthracothorax nigricollis (Vieillot, 1817)

Spanish: Mango Gorjinegro

The Black-throated Mango (Anthracothorax nigricollis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 7.0 g and its bill is 25.7 mm long. It lives in 14 countries, including Colombia, Brazil, Panama, Ecuador and Trinidad and Tobago. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
