White-tailed Emerald

Microchera chionura (Gould, 1851)

Spanish: Esmeralda de Elvira

The White-tailed Emerald (Microchera chionura) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 17.7 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
