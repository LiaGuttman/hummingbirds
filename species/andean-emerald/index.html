Andean Emerald

Uranomitra franciae (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1846)

Spanish: Amazilia Andina

The Andean Emerald (Uranomitra franciae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 24.9 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Ecuador and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
