Green-crowned Plovercrest

Stephanoxis lalandi (Vieillot, 1818)

Spanish: Colibrí Copetón Norteño

The Green-crowned Plovercrest (Stephanoxis lalandi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.0 g and its bill is 17.2 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
