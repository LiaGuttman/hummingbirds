Red-tailed Comet

Sappho sparganurus (Shaw, 1812)

Spanish: Colibrí Cometa

The Red-tailed Comet (Sappho sparganurus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.3 g and its bill is 20.1 mm long. It lives in Argentina and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
