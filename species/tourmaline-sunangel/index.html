Tourmaline Sunangel

Heliangelus exortis (Fraser, 1840)

Spanish: Colibrí Turmalina

The Tourmaline Sunangel (Heliangelus exortis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 4.6 g and its bill is 19.8 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
