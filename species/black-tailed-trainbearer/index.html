Black-tailed Trainbearer

Lesbia victoriae (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1846)

Spanish: Colibrí Colilargo Mayor

The Black-tailed Trainbearer (Lesbia victoriae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 18.6 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
