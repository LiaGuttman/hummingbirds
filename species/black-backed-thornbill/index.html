Black-backed Thornbill

Ramphomicron dorsale Salvin & Godman, 1880

Spanish: Colibrí Piquicorto Dorsinegro

The Black-backed Thornbill (Ramphomicron dorsale) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.5 g and its bill is 12.7 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
