Venezuelan Sylph

Aglaiocercus berlepschi (Hartert, EJO, 1898)

Spanish: Silfo de Berlepsch

The Venezuelan Sylph (Aglaiocercus berlepschi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 18.4 mm long. It is found only in Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Vulnerable, with a decreasing population.
