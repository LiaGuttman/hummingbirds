Blue-throated Mountain-gem

Lampornis clemenciae (Lesson, RP, 1830)

Spanish: Colibrí Garganta Azul · IOC: Blue-throated mountaingem

The Blue-throated Mountain-gem (Lampornis clemenciae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 7.6 g and its bill is 28.4 mm long. It lives in United States and Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
