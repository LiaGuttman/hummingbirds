Blue-capped Puffleg

Eriocnemis glaucopoides (d'Orbigny & Lafresnaye, 1838)

Spanish: Calzadito Frentiazul

The Blue-capped Puffleg (Eriocnemis glaucopoides) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 3.7 g and its bill is 25.0 mm long. It lives in Argentina and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
