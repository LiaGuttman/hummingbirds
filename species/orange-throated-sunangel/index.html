Orange-throated Sunangel

Heliangelus mavors Gould, 1848

Spanish: Colibrí de Marte

The Orange-throated Sunangel (Heliangelus mavors) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 4.2 g and its bill is 18.2 mm long. It lives in Venezuela and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
