Green-breasted Mango

Anthracothorax prevostii (Lesson, RP, 1832)

Spanish: Colibrí Garganta Negra

The Green-breasted Mango (Anthracothorax prevostii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 6.4 g and its bill is 28.9 mm long. It lives in 11 countries, including Costa Rica, Belize, Mexico, Honduras and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
