Scissor-tailed Hummingbird

Hylonympha macrocerca Gould, 1873

Spanish: Colibrí Tijereta

The Scissor-tailed Hummingbird (Hylonympha macrocerca) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 7.3 g and its bill is 27.3 mm long. It is found only in Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
