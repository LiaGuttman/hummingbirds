Short-tailed Woodstar

Myrmia micrura (Gould, 1854)

Spanish: Colibrí Colicorto

The Short-tailed Woodstar (Myrmia micrura) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.3 g and its bill is 15.8 mm long. It lives in Peru and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
