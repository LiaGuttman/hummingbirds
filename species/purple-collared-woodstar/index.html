Purple-collared Woodstar

Myrtis fanny (Lesson, RP, 1838)

Spanish: Colibrí de Myrtis

The Purple-collared Woodstar (Myrtis fanny) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.3 g and its bill is 20.9 mm long. It lives in Peru and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
