Stripe-breasted Starthroat

Heliomaster squamosus (Temminck, 1823)

Spanish: Colibrí Escamoso

The Stripe-breasted Starthroat (Heliomaster squamosus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 5.9 g and its bill is 32.4 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
