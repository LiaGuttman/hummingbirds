Black-chinned Hummingbird

Archilochus alexandri (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1846)

Spanish: Colibrí Barba Negra

The Black-chinned Hummingbird (Archilochus alexandri) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 3.4 g and its bill is 21.6 mm long. It lives in the United States, Canada and Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a increasing population.
