White-bellied Mountain-gem

Lampornis hemileucus (Salvin, 1865)

Spanish: Colibrí Gorjivioleta · IOC: White-bellied mountaingem

The White-bellied Mountain-gem (Lampornis hemileucus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 5.5 g and its bill is 22.9 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
