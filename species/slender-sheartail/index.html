Slender Sheartail

Doricha enicura (Vieillot, 1818)

Spanish: Colibrí Tijereta Guatemalteco

The Slender Sheartail (Doricha enicura) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.4 g and its bill is 21.9 mm long. It lives in Guatemala, Mexico, Honduras and El Salvador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
