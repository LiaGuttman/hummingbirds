Sooty Barbthroat

Threnetes niger (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Ermitaño Barbudo Guayanés

The Sooty Barbthroat (Threnetes niger) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.6 g and its bill is 33.4 mm long. It lives in French Guiana and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
