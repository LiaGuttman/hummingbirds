Reddish Hermit

Phaethornis ruber (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Ermitaño Rojizo

The Reddish Hermit (Phaethornis ruber) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 2.4 g and its bill is 24.0 mm long. It lives in 9 countries, including Brazil, Peru, Colombia, Guyana and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
