White-throated Hummingbird

Leucochloris albicollis (Vieillot, 1818)

Spanish: Colibrí Gargantilla

The White-throated Hummingbird (Leucochloris albicollis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 6.3 g and its bill is 26.9 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay and Paraguay. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
