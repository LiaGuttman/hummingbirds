Wedge-tailed Sabrewing

Pampa curvipennis (Deppe, 1830)

Spanish: Fandanguero Mexicano · IOC: Curve-winged sabrewing

The Wedge-tailed Sabrewing (Pampa curvipennis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 6.0 g and its bill is 29.0 mm long. It lives in Mexico, Belize, Guatemala and Honduras. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
