Turquoise-throated Puffleg

Eriocnemis godini (Bourcier, 1851)

Spanish: Calzadito Turquesa

The Turquoise-throated Puffleg (Eriocnemis godini) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 5.1 g and its bill is 23.4 mm long. Its IUCN Red List status is Critically endangered.
