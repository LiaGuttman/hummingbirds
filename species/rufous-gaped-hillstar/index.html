Rufous-gaped Hillstar

Urochroa bougueri (Bourcier, 1851)

Spanish: Colibrí de Bouguer Occidental

The Rufous-gaped Hillstar (Urochroa bougueri) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 11.4 g and its bill is 33.3 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
