Spangled Coquette

Lophornis stictolophus Salvin & Elliot, DG, 1873

Spanish: Coqueta Coronada

The Spangled Coquette (Lophornis stictolophus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 2.7 g and its bill is 12.8 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Venezuela and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
