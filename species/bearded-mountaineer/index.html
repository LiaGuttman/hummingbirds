Bearded Mountaineer

Oreonympha nobilis Gould, 1869

Spanish: Colibrí Noble

The Bearded Mountaineer (Oreonympha nobilis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 7.7 g and its bill is 27.4 mm long. It is found only in Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
