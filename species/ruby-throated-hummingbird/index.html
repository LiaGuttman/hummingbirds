Ruby-throated Hummingbird

Archilochus colubris (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Colibrí Garganta Rubí

The Ruby-throated Hummingbird (Archilochus colubris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 3.1 g and its bill is 20.7 mm long. It lives in 12 countries, including the United States, Canada, Mexico, Costa Rica and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a increasing population.

What Archilochus share: Two species, the Ruby-throated and the Black-chinned Hummingbird, and both migrate. Some Ruby-throats fly nonstop across the Gulf of Mexico, about 20 hours over open water.
