Puerto Rican Mango

Anthracothorax aurulentus (Audebert & Vieillot, 1801)

Spanish: Mango Puertoriqueño Dorado

The Puerto Rican Mango (Anthracothorax aurulentus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 5.8 g and its bill is 27.5 mm long. It lives in Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands and Virgin Islands. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
