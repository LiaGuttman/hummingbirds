Andean Hillstar

Oreotrochilus estella (d'Orbigny, 1838)

Spanish: Colibrí Puneño

The Andean Hillstar (Oreotrochilus estella) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 8.1 g and its bill is 25.0 mm long. It lives in Chile, Peru, Bolivia and Argentina. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
