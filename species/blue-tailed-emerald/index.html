Blue-tailed Emerald

Chlorostilbon mellisugus (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Esmeralda Coliazul

The Blue-tailed Emerald (Chlorostilbon mellisugus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 2.6 g and its bill is 19.2 mm long. It lives in 13 countries, including Venezuela, Peru, Curaçao, Colombia and Aruba. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
