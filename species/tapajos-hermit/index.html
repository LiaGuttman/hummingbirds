Tapajos Hermit

Phaethornis aethopygus Zimmer, JT, 1950

Spanish: Ermitaño de Tapajoz

The Tapajos Hermit (Phaethornis aethopygus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 26.0 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Vulnerable, with a decreasing population.
