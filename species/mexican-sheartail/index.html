Mexican Sheartail

Doricha eliza (Lesson, RP & Delattre, 1839)

Spanish: Colibrí Tijereta Mexicano

The Mexican Sheartail (Doricha eliza) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.5 g and its bill is 23.1 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
