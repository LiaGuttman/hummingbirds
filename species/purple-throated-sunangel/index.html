Purple-throated Sunangel

Heliangelus viola Gould, 1853

Spanish: Colibrí Violeta

The Purple-throated Sunangel (Heliangelus viola) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.4 g and its bill is 19.3 mm long. It lives in Ecuador and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
