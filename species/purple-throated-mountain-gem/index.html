Purple-throated Mountain-gem

Lampornis calolaemus (Salvin, 1865)

Spanish: Colibrí Gorjipúrpura · IOC: Purple-throated mountaingem

The Purple-throated Mountain-gem (Lampornis calolaemus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 5.1 g and its bill is 23.5 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica, Panama and Nicaragua. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
