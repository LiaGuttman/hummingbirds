Stripe-throated Hermit

Phaethornis striigularis Gould, 1854

Spanish: Colibrí Ermitaño Enano

The Stripe-throated Hermit (Phaethornis striigularis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 23.7 mm long. It lives in 10 countries, including Costa Rica, Panama, Belize, Guatemala and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
