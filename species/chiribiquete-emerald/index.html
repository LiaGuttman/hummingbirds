Chiribiquete Emerald

Chlorostilbon olivaresi Stiles, 1996

Spanish: Esmeralda del Chiribiquete

The Chiribiquete Emerald (Chlorostilbon olivaresi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.7 g and its bill is 22.2 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
