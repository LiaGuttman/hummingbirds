Steely-vented Hummingbird

Saucerottia saucerottei (Delattre & Bourcier, 1846)

Spanish: Amazilia Verdiazul

The Steely-vented Hummingbird (Saucerottia saucerottei) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 20.7 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
