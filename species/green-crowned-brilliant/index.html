Green-crowned Brilliant

Heliodoxa jacula Gould, 1850

Spanish: Brillante Coroniverde

The Green-crowned Brilliant (Heliodoxa jacula) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 8.1 g and its bill is 26.4 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica, Ecuador, Colombia and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
