Blue-fronted Lancebill

Doryfera johannae (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Colibrí Picolanza Menor

The Blue-fronted Lancebill (Doryfera johannae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 4.8 g and its bill is 30.6 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Peru, Colombia, Venezuela, Guyana and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
