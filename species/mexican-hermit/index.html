Mexican Hermit

Phaethornis mexicanus Hartert, EJO, 1897

Spanish: Colibrí Ermitaño Mexicano

The Mexican Hermit (Phaethornis mexicanus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 6.0 g and its bill is 46.4 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
