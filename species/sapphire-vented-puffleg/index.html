Sapphire-vented Puffleg

Eriocnemis luciani (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Calzadito Colilargo

The Sapphire-vented Puffleg (Eriocnemis luciani) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.0 g and its bill is 24.6 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Peru and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
