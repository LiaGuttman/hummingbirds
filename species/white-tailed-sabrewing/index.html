White-tailed Sabrewing

Campylopterus ensipennis (Swainson, 1822)

Spanish: Colibrí Coliblanco

The White-tailed Sabrewing (Campylopterus ensipennis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 9.6 g and its bill is 29.5 mm long. It lives in Trinidad and Tobago and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
