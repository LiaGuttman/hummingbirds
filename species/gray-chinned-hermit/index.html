Gray-chinned Hermit

Phaethornis griseogularis Gould, 1851

Spanish: Ermitaño Barbigrís · IOC: Grey-chinned hermit

The Gray-chinned Hermit (Phaethornis griseogularis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 2.3 g and its bill is 25.3 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
