Sapphire-bellied Hummingbird

Chrysuronia lilliae (Stone, 1917)

Spanish: Colibrí Ventrizafiro

The Sapphire-bellied Hummingbird (Chrysuronia lilliae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.3 g and its bill is 21.2 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
