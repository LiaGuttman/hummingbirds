Allen's Hummingbird

Selasphorus sasin (Lesson, RP, 1829)

Spanish: Zumbador de Allen

The Allen's Hummingbird (Selasphorus sasin) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 3.4 g and its bill is 20.2 mm long. It lives in the United States and Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a increasing population.
