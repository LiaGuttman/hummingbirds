Pirre Hummingbird

Goldmania bella (Nelson, 1912)

Spanish: Colibrí del Pirre

The Pirre Hummingbird (Goldmania bella) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.4 g and its bill is 20.2 mm long. It lives in Panama and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened.
