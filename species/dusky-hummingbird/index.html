Dusky Hummingbird

Phaeoptila sordida (Gould, 1859)

Spanish: Colibrí Opaco

The Dusky Hummingbird (Phaeoptila sordida) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.0 g and its bill is 23.4 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
