Purple-crowned Plovercrest

Stephanoxis loddigesii (Vigors, 1831)

Spanish: Colibrí Copetón Sureño

The Purple-crowned Plovercrest (Stephanoxis loddigesii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.0 g and its bill is 19.1 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Argentina and Paraguay. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
