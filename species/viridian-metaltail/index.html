Viridian Metaltail

Metallura williami (Delattre & Bourcier, 1846)

Spanish: Metalura Verde

The Viridian Metaltail (Metallura williami) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 16.2 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
