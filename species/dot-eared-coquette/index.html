Dot-eared Coquette

Lophornis gouldii (Lesson, RP, 1832)

Spanish: Coqueta Moteada

The Dot-eared Coquette (Lophornis gouldii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 2.4 g and its bill is 13.3 mm long. It lives in Brazil and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
