Sapphire-throated Hummingbird

Chrysuronia coeruleogularis (Gould, 1851)

Spanish: Colibrí Gorjizafiro

The Sapphire-throated Hummingbird (Chrysuronia coeruleogularis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.2 g and its bill is 21.5 mm long. It lives in Panama, Colombia and Costa Rica. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
