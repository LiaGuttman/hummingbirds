Glow-throated Hummingbird

Selasphorus ardens Salvin, 1870

Spanish: Colibrí Ardiente

The Glow-throated Hummingbird (Selasphorus ardens) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 3.1 g and its bill is 14.3 mm long. It is found only in Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
