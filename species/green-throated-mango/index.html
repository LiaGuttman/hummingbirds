Green-throated Mango

Anthracothorax viridigula (Boddaert, 1783)

Spanish: Mango Gorjiverde

The Green-throated Mango (Anthracothorax viridigula) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 7.5 g and its bill is 32.0 mm long. It lives in Trinidad and Tobago, Brazil, Suriname, Guyana, French Guiana and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
