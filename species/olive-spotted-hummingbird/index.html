Olive-spotted Hummingbird

Talaphorus chlorocercus (Gould, 1866)

Spanish: Colibrí Blanquioliva

The Olive-spotted Hummingbird (Talaphorus chlorocercus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.6 g and its bill is 19.6 mm long. It lives in Peru, Ecuador, Colombia and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
