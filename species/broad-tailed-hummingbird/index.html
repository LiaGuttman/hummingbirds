Broad-tailed Hummingbird

Selasphorus platycercus (Swainson, 1827)

Spanish: Zumbador Cola Ancha

The Broad-tailed Hummingbird (Selasphorus platycercus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 3.5 g and its bill is 21.1 mm long. It lives in United States, Mexico and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
