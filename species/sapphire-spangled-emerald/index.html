Sapphire-spangled Emerald

Chionomesa lactea (Lesson, RP, 1832)

Spanish: Amazilia Zafirina

The Sapphire-spangled Emerald (Chionomesa lactea) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.6 g and its bill is 22.5 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Peru, Bolivia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
