Straight-billed Hermit

Phaethornis bourcieri (Lesson, RP, 1832)

Spanish: Ermitaño Piquirrecto

The Straight-billed Hermit (Phaethornis bourcieri) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 4.3 g and its bill is 34.0 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil, Suriname, Peru, Venezuela, French Guiana and Guyana. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
