Violet-capped Woodnymph

Thalurania glaucopis (Gmelin, JF, 1788)

Spanish: Zafiro Capirotado

The Violet-capped Woodnymph (Thalurania glaucopis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.8 g and its bill is 22.8 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Argentina and Paraguay. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
