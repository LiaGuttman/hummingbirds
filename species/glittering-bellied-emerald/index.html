Glittering-bellied Emerald

Chlorostilbon lucidus (Shaw, 1812)

Spanish: Esmeralda Ventridorada

The Glittering-bellied Emerald (Chlorostilbon lucidus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.5 g and its bill is 17.4 mm long. It lives in Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Paraguay and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
