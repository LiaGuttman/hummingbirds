Bronzy Inca

Coeligena coeligena (Lesson, RP, 1833)

Spanish: Inca Bronceado

The Bronzy Inca (Coeligena coeligena) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.8 g and its bill is 36.2 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
