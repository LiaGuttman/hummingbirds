White-tailed Hummingbird

Eupherusa poliocerca Elliot, DG, 1871

Spanish: Colibrí Guerrerense

The White-tailed Hummingbird (Eupherusa poliocerca) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.8 g and its bill is 21.5 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
