Lazuline Sabrewing

Campylopterus falcatus (Swainson, 1821)

Spanish: Colibrí Lazulita

The Lazuline Sabrewing (Campylopterus falcatus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 7.2 g and its bill is 27.0 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Venezuela and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
