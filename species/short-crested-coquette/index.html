Short-crested Coquette

Lophornis brachylophus Moore, RT, 1949

Spanish: Coqueta de Atoyac

The Short-crested Coquette (Lophornis brachylophus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 2.7 g and its bill is 12.9 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Critically endangered, with a decreasing population.
