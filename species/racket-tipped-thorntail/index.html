Racket-tipped Thorntail

Discosura longicaudus (Gmelin, JF, 1788)

Spanish: Rabudito de Raquetas

The Racket-tipped Thorntail (Discosura longicaudus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.4 g and its bill is 15.4 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Suriname, Guyana, Venezuela and French Guiana. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
