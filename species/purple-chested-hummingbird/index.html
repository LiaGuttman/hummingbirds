Purple-chested Hummingbird

Polyerata rosenbergi Boucard, 1895

Spanish: Amazilia de Rosenberg

The Purple-chested Hummingbird (Polyerata rosenbergi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.7 g and its bill is 23.9 mm long. It lives in Ecuador and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
