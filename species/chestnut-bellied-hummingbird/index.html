Chestnut-bellied Hummingbird

Saucerottia castaneiventris (Gould, 1856)

Spanish: Amazilia Ventricastaña

The Chestnut-bellied Hummingbird (Saucerottia castaneiventris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 21.9 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
