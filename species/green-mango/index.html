Green Mango

Anthracothorax viridis (Audebert & Vieillot, 1801)

Spanish: Mango Puertorriqueño Verde

The Green Mango (Anthracothorax viridis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 6.6 g and its bill is 26.2 mm long. It is found only in Puerto Rico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
