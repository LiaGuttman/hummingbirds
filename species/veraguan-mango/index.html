Veraguan Mango

Anthracothorax veraguensis Reichenbach, 1855

Spanish: Mango de Veragua

The Veraguan Mango (Anthracothorax veraguensis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 7.0 g and its bill is 27.7 mm long. It lives in Panama and Costa Rica. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
