Green-backed Firecrown

Sephanoides sephaniodes (Lesson, RP & Garnot, 1827)

Spanish: Colibrí Austral

The Green-backed Firecrown (Sephanoides sephaniodes) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.2 g and its bill is 22.0 mm long. It lives in Chile and Argentina. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
