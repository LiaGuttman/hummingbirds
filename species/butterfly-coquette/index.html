Butterfly Coquette

Lophornis verreauxii Bourcier, 1853

Spanish: Coqueta Verde Norteña

The Butterfly Coquette (Lophornis verreauxii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 17.5 mm long. It lives in Peru, Colombia, Brazil, Bolivia, Ecuador and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
