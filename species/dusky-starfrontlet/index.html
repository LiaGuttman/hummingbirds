Dusky Starfrontlet

Coeligena orina Wetmore, 1953

Spanish: Inca de Antioquía

The Dusky Starfrontlet (Coeligena orina) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.5 g and its bill is 37.2 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
