Amazilia Hummingbird

Amazilis amazilia (Lesson, RP & Garnot, 1827)

Spanish: Amazilia Costeña

The Amazilia Hummingbird (Amazilis amazilia) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.3 g and its bill is 20.7 mm long. It lives in Peru and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
