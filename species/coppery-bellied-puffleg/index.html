Coppery-bellied Puffleg

Eriocnemis cupreoventris (Fraser, 1840)

Spanish: Calzadito Cobrizo

The Coppery-bellied Puffleg (Eriocnemis cupreoventris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 5.3 g and its bill is 21.3 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
