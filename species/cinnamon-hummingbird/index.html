Cinnamon Hummingbird

Amazilia rutila (Delattre, 1843)

Spanish: Colibrí Canelo

The Cinnamon Hummingbird (Amazilia rutila) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 5.2 g and its bill is 24.5 mm long. It lives in Mexico, Costa Rica, Honduras, El Salvador, Nicaragua, Belize and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
