Antillean Crested Hummingbird

Orthorhyncus cristatus (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Colibrí Crestado

The Antillean Crested Hummingbird (Orthorhyncus cristatus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 15.7 mm long. It lives in 18 countries, including Guadeloupe, Grenada, Saint Lucia, Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
