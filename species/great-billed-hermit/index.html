Great-billed Hermit

Phaethornis malaris (Nordmann, 1835)

Spanish: Ermitaño Picogrande

The Great-billed Hermit (Phaethornis malaris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.9 g and its bill is 44.3 mm long. It lives in Peru, Ecuador, Colombia, Bolivia, Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
