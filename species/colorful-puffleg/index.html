Colorful Puffleg

Eriocnemis mirabilis Meyer de Schauensee, 1967

Spanish: Calzadito Admirable

The Colorful Puffleg (Eriocnemis mirabilis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 5.1 g and its bill is 20.3 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
