Black-throated Hermit

Phaethornis atrimentalis Lawrence, 1858

Spanish: Ermitaño Barbinegro

The Black-throated Hermit (Phaethornis atrimentalis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 2.6 g and its bill is 25.2 mm long. It lives in Peru, Ecuador and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
