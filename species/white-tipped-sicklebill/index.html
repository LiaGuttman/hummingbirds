White-tipped Sicklebill

Eutoxeres aquila (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Picohoz Coliverde

The White-tipped Sicklebill (Eutoxeres aquila) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 10.6 g and its bill is 27.5 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica, Ecuador, Colombia, Panama and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
