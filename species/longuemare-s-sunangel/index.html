Longuemare's Sunangel

Heliangelus clarisse (Longuemare, 1841)

Spanish: Colibrí de Clarissa

The Longuemare's Sunangel (Heliangelus clarisse) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 20.6 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
