Green-throated Carib

Eulampis holosericeus (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Colibrí Caribeño Gorjiverde

The Green-throated Carib (Eulampis holosericeus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 5.7 g and its bill is 25.4 mm long. It lives in 18 countries, including Puerto Rico, Guadeloupe, Virgin Islands, Saint Lucia and Barbados. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
