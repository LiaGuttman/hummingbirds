White-chested Emerald

Chrysuronia brevirostris (Lesson, RP, 1829)

Spanish: Amazilia Pechiblanca

The White-chested Emerald (Chrysuronia brevirostris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.3 g and its bill is 19.6 mm long. It lives in Trinidad and Tobago, Guyana, Venezuela, Brazil, Suriname and French Guiana. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
