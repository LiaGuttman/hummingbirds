Bumblebee Hummingbird

Selasphorus heloisa (Lesson, RP & Delattre, 1839)

Spanish: Zumbador Mexicano

The Bumblebee Hummingbird (Selasphorus heloisa) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.2 g and its bill is 14.7 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
