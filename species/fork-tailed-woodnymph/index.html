Fork-tailed Woodnymph

Thalurania furcata (Gmelin, JF, 1788)

Spanish: Zafiro Golondrina

The Fork-tailed Woodnymph (Thalurania furcata) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.2 g and its bill is 24.0 mm long. It lives in 11 countries, including Brazil, Peru, Ecuador, Colombia and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
