Black-breasted Puffleg

Eriocnemis nigrivestis (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1852)

Spanish: Calzadito Pechinegro

The Black-breasted Puffleg (Eriocnemis nigrivestis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 8.5 g and its bill is 19.3 mm long. It is found only in Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
