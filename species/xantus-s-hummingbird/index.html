Xantus's Hummingbird

Basilinna xantusii (Lawrence, 1860)

Spanish: Colibrí Bajacaliforniano

The Xantus's Hummingbird (Basilinna xantusii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.4 g and its bill is 17.8 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
