Geoffroy's Daggerbill

Schistes geoffroyi (Bourcier, 1843)

Spanish: Colibrí Picocuña Oriental

The Geoffroy's Daggerbill (Schistes geoffroyi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 3.6 g and its bill is 17.1 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Peru, Colombia, Venezuela and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
