Tufted Coquette

Lophornis ornatus (Boddaert, 1783)

Spanish: Coqueta Adornada

The Tufted Coquette (Lophornis ornatus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 2.3 g and its bill is 12.0 mm long. It lives in Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela, Suriname, French Guiana, Guyana and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
