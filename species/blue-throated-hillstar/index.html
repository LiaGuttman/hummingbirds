Blue-throated Hillstar

Oreotrochilus cyanolaemus Sornoza-Molina, Freile, Nilsson, J, Krabbe & Bonaccorso, 2018

Spanish: Colibrí del Arcos

The Blue-throated Hillstar (Oreotrochilus cyanolaemus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 8.1 g and its bill is 22.7 mm long. It is found only in Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Critically endangered, with a decreasing population.
