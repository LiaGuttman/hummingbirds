Fawn-breasted Brilliant

Heliodoxa rubinoides (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1846)

Spanish: Brillante Pechigamuza

The Fawn-breasted Brilliant (Heliodoxa rubinoides) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 7.8 g and its bill is 27.7 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
