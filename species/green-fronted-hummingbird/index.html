Green-fronted Hummingbird

Ramosomyia viridifrons (Elliot, DG, 1871)

Spanish: Colibrí Frente Verde

The Green-fronted Hummingbird (Ramosomyia viridifrons) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 6.3 g and its bill is 23.9 mm long. It lives in Mexico and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
