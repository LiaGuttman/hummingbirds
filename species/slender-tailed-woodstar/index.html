Slender-tailed Woodstar

Microstilbon burmeisteri (Sclater, PL, 1888)

Spanish: Colibrí de Burmeister

The Slender-tailed Woodstar (Microstilbon burmeisteri) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.5 g and its bill is 17.5 mm long. It lives in Argentina and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
