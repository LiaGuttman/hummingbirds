Volcano Hummingbird

Selasphorus flammula Salvin, 1865

Spanish: Colibrí Volcanero

The Volcano Hummingbird (Selasphorus flammula) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.7 g and its bill is 14.5 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
