Amethyst-throated Sunangel

Heliangelus amethysticollis (d'Orbigny & Lafresnaye, 1838)

Spanish: Colibrí Gorjiamatista

The Amethyst-throated Sunangel (Heliangelus amethysticollis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 20.7 mm long. It lives in Peru, Ecuador and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
