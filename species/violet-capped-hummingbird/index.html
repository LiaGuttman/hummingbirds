Violet-capped Hummingbird

Goldmania violiceps Nelson, 1911

Spanish: Colibrí de Goldman

The Violet-capped Hummingbird (Goldmania violiceps) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.1 g and its bill is 21.7 mm long. It lives in Panama and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
