Black-bellied Hummingbird

Eupherusa nigriventris Lawrence, 1868

Spanish: Colibrí Ventrinegro

The Black-bellied Hummingbird (Eupherusa nigriventris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.5 g and its bill is 16.1 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
