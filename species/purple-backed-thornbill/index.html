Purple-backed Thornbill

Ramphomicron microrhynchum (Boissonneau, 1840)

Spanish: Colibrí Piquicorto Común

The Purple-backed Thornbill (Ramphomicron microrhynchum) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.6 g and its bill is 11.1 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
