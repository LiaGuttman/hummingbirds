Turquoise-crowned Hummingbird

Cynanthus doubledayi (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Colibrí Pico Ancho Mexicano

The Turquoise-crowned Hummingbird (Cynanthus doubledayi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.1 g and its bill is 18.6 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
