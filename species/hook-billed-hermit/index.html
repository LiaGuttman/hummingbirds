Hook-billed Hermit

Glaucis dohrnii (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1852)

Spanish: Ermitaño de Espírito Santo

The Hook-billed Hermit (Glaucis dohrnii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.8 g and its bill is 28.4 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Vulnerable, with a decreasing population.
