Green-fronted Lancebill

Doryfera ludovicae (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1847)

Spanish: Colibrí Picolanza Mayor

The Green-fronted Lancebill (Doryfera ludovicae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 6.0 g and its bill is 37.6 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Peru, Panama, Bolivia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
