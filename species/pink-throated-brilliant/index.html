Pink-throated Brilliant

Heliodoxa gularis (Gould, 1860)

Spanish: Brillante Gorjirrosado

The Pink-throated Brilliant (Heliodoxa gularis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.3 g and its bill is 28.0 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Peru and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
