Hoary Puffleg

Haplophaedia lugens (Gould, 1852)

Spanish: Calzadito Canoso

The Hoary Puffleg (Haplophaedia lugens) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.9 g and its bill is 19.2 mm long. It lives in Ecuador and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a stable population.
