Green-tailed Trainbearer

Lesbia nuna (Lesson, RP, 1832)

Spanish: Colibrí Colilargo Menor

The Green-tailed Trainbearer (Lesbia nuna) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.7 g and its bill is 17.9 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Peru, Ecuador and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
