Blue-vented Hummingbird

Saucerottia hoffmanni (Cabanis & Heine, 1860)

Spanish: Amazilia Azul

The Blue-vented Hummingbird (Saucerottia hoffmanni) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 21.8 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica and Nicaragua. Its IUCN Red List status is Not evaluated.
