Honduran Emerald

Amazilia luciae (Lawrence, 1868)

Spanish: Amazilia Hondureña

The Honduran Emerald (Amazilia luciae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 23.0 mm long. It is found only in Honduras. Its IUCN Red List status is Vulnerable, with a decreasing population.
