Peacock Coquette

Lophornis pavoninus Salvin & Godman, 1882

Spanish: Coqueta Pavonina

The Peacock Coquette (Lophornis pavoninus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 13.7 mm long. It lives in Venezuela, Guyana and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
