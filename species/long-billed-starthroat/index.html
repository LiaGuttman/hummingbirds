Long-billed Starthroat

Heliomaster longirostris (Audebert & Vieillot, 1801)

Spanish: Colibrí Picudo Coroniazul

The Long-billed Starthroat (Heliomaster longirostris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 6.6 g and its bill is 35.0 mm long. It lives in 17 countries, including Costa Rica, Ecuador, Panama, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
