Buff-bellied Hummingbird

Amazilia yucatanensis (Cabot, S, 1845)

Spanish: Colibrí Vientre Canelo

The Buff-bellied Hummingbird (Amazilia yucatanensis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.9 g and its bill is 23.7 mm long. It lives in the United States, Mexico, Belize and Guatemala. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
