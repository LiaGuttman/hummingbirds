Garnet-throated Hummingbird

Lamprolaima rhami (Lesson, RP, 1839)

Spanish: Colibrí Multicolor

The Garnet-throated Hummingbird (Lamprolaima rhami) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 7.8 g and its bill is 21.1 mm long. It lives in Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras and El Salvador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
