Inagua Woodstar

Nesophlox lyrura (Gould, 1869)

Spanish: Colibrí de Inagua

The Inagua Woodstar (Nesophlox lyrura) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.4 g and its bill is 17.7 mm long. It is found only in the Bahamas. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
