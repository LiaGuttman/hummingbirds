Green-bearded Helmetcrest

Oxypogon guerinii (Boissonneau, 1840)

Spanish: Colibrí Chivito de Bogotá

The Green-bearded Helmetcrest (Oxypogon guerinii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 4.8 g and its bill is 12.7 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
