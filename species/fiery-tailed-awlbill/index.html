Fiery-tailed Awlbill

Avocettula recurvirostris (Swainson, 1822)

Spanish: Mango Picolezna

The Fiery-tailed Awlbill (Avocettula recurvirostris) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 4.2 g and its bill is 18.6 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Guyana, French Guiana, Ecuador, Venezuela and Suriname. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
