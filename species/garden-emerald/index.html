Garden Emerald

Chlorostilbon assimilis Lawrence, 1861

Spanish: Esmeralda Afín

The Garden Emerald (Chlorostilbon assimilis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 2.6 g and its bill is 16.4 mm long. It lives in Panama and Costa Rica. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
