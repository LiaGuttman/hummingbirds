Mangrove Hummingbird

Chrysuronia boucardi (Mulsant, 1877)

Spanish: Amazilia de Manglar

The Mangrove Hummingbird (Chrysuronia boucardi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 22.1 mm long. It is found only in Costa Rica. Its IUCN Red List status is Endangered, with a decreasing population.
