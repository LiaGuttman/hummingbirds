Bronze-tailed Comet

Polyonymus caroli (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Colibrí Colibronce

The Bronze-tailed Comet (Polyonymus caroli) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 4.8 g and its bill is 23.2 mm long. It is found only in Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
