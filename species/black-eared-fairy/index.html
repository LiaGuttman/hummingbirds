Black-eared Fairy

Heliothryx auritus (Gmelin, JF, 1788)

Spanish: Colibrí Hada Oriental

The Black-eared Fairy (Heliothryx auritus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 5.4 g and its bill is 24.1 mm long. It lives in 9 countries, including Brazil, Ecuador, Peru, Colombia and Guyana. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
