Coppery-headed Emerald

Microchera cupreiceps (Lawrence, 1866)

Spanish: Esmeralda Capirotada

The Coppery-headed Emerald (Microchera cupreiceps) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 16.9 mm long. It is found only in Costa Rica. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
