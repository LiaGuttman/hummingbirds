Amethyst Woodstar

Calliphlox amethystina (Boddaert, 1783)

Spanish: Colibrí Amatista

The Amethyst Woodstar (Calliphlox amethystina) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 2.8 g and its bill is 19.3 mm long. It lives in 11 countries, including Brazil, Peru, Venezuela, Ecuador and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
