Scaly-breasted Hummingbird

Phaeochroa cuvierii (Delattre & Bourcier, 1846)

Spanish: Colibrí Pecho Escamoso

The Scaly-breasted Hummingbird (Phaeochroa cuvierii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 8.9 g and its bill is 24.2 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica, Belize, Panama, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
