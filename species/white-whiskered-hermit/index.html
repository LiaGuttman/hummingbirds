White-whiskered Hermit

Phaethornis yaruqui (Bourcier, 1851)

Spanish: Ermitaño de Yaruquí

The White-whiskered Hermit (Phaethornis yaruqui) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.4 g and its bill is 45.6 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
