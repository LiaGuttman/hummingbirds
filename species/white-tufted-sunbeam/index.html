White-tufted Sunbeam

Aglaeactis castelnaudii (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1848)

Spanish: Colibrí Condecorado

The White-tufted Sunbeam (Aglaeactis castelnaudii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.7 g and its bill is 22.9 mm long. It is found only in Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
