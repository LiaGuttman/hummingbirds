Great Sapphirewing

Pterophanes cyanopterus (Fraser, 1840)

Spanish: Colibrí Aliazul

The Great Sapphirewing (Pterophanes cyanopterus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 9.3 g and its bill is 37.9 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
