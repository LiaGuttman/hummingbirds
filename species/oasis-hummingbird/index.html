Oasis Hummingbird

Rhodopis vesper (Lesson, RP, 1829)

Spanish: Colibrí del Atacama

The Oasis Hummingbird (Rhodopis vesper) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 3.8 g and its bill is 34.8 mm long. It lives in Chile and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
