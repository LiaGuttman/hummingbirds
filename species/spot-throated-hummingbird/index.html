Spot-throated Hummingbird

Thaumasius taczanowskii Sclater, PL, 1879

Spanish: Colibrí de Taczanowski

The Spot-throated Hummingbird (Thaumasius taczanowskii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 6.4 g and its bill is 27.7 mm long. It lives in Peru and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
