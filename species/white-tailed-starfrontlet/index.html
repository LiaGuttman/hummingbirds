White-tailed Starfrontlet

Coeligena phalerata (Bangs, 1898)

Spanish: Inca Coliblanco

The White-tailed Starfrontlet (Coeligena phalerata) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 6.1 g and its bill is 33.4 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
