White-bearded Hermit

Phaethornis hispidus (Gould, 1846)

Spanish: Ermitaño Barbiblanco

The White-bearded Hermit (Phaethornis hispidus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 34.4 mm long. It lives in Peru, Colombia, Ecuador, Brazil, Bolivia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
