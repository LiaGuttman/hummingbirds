Humboldt's Sapphire

Chrysuronia humboldtii (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1852)

Spanish: Amazilia de Humboldt

The Humboldt's Sapphire (Chrysuronia humboldtii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 5.6 g and its bill is 21.0 mm long. It lives in Colombia, Panama and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
