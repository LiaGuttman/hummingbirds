Indigo-capped Hummingbird

Saucerottia cyanifrons (Bourcier, 1843)

Spanish: Amazilia Capiazul

The Indigo-capped Hummingbird (Saucerottia cyanifrons) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 5.0 g and its bill is 20.3 mm long. It is found only in Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
