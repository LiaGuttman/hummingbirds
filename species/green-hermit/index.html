Green Hermit

Phaethornis guy (Lesson, RP, 1833)

Spanish: Ermitaño Verde

The Green Hermit (Phaethornis guy) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 6.3 g and its bill is 42.4 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica, Colombia, Panama, Ecuador, Trinidad and Tobago, Peru and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
