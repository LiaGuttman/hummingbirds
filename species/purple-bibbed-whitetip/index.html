Purple-bibbed Whitetip

Urosticte benjamini (Bourcier, 1851)

Spanish: Colibrí Puntiblanco Occidental

The Purple-bibbed Whitetip (Urosticte benjamini) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 3.9 g and its bill is 20.7 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Colombia and Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
