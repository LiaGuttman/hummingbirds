Mexican Violetear

Colibri thalassinus (Swainson, 1827)

Spanish: Colibrí Orejas Violetas Mexicano

The Mexican Violetear (Colibri thalassinus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 5.9 g and its bill is 23.9 mm long. It lives in Mexico, Guatemala, Honduras, El Salvador, United States and Nicaragua. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
