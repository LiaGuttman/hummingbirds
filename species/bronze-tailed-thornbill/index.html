Bronze-tailed Thornbill

Chalcostigma heteropogon (Boissonneau, 1840)

Spanish: Colibrí Picoespina

The Bronze-tailed Thornbill (Chalcostigma heteropogon) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 6.1 g and its bill is 15.8 mm long. It lives in Colombia and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
