Emerald-chinned Hummingbird

Abeillia abeillei (Lesson, RP & Delattre, 1839)

Spanish: Colibrí Pico Corto

The Emerald-chinned Hummingbird (Abeillia abeillei) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 2.7 g and its bill is 13.0 mm long. It lives in Guatemala, Mexico, Nicaragua, Honduras and El Salvador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
