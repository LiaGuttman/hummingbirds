White-bearded Helmetcrest

Oxypogon lindenii (Parzudaki, 1845)

Spanish: Colibrí Chivito de Mérida

The White-bearded Helmetcrest (Oxypogon lindenii) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 4.8 g and its bill is 13.6 mm long. It is found only in Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened.
