Mexican Woodnymph

Eupherusa ridgwayi (Nelson, 1900)

Spanish: Ninfa Mexicana

The Mexican Woodnymph (Eupherusa ridgwayi) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.8 g and its bill is 20.8 mm long. It is found only in Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Vulnerable, with a decreasing population.
