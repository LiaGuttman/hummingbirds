Buff-breasted Sabrewing

Campylopterus duidae Chapman, 1929

Spanish: Colibrí del Duida

The Buff-breasted Sabrewing (Campylopterus duidae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 6.1 g and its bill is 28.0 mm long. It lives in Venezuela and Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
