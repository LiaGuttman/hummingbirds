Broad-tipped Hermit

Anopetia gounellei (Boucard, 1891)

Spanish: Ermitaño Coliancho

The Broad-tipped Hermit (Anopetia gounellei) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 3.2 g and its bill is 25.4 mm long. It is found only in Brazil. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
