Jamaican Mango

Anthracothorax mango (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Mango Jamaicano

The Jamaican Mango (Anthracothorax mango) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 8.3 g and its bill is 28.6 mm long. It is found only in Jamaica. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
