Bronze-tailed Plumeleteer

Chalybura urochrysia (Gould, 1861)

Spanish: Colibrí Patirrojo

The Bronze-tailed Plumeleteer (Chalybura urochrysia) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 6.4 g and its bill is 26.2 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica, Panama, Colombia, Honduras, Nicaragua and Ecuador. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
