Brace's Emerald

Riccordia bracei (Lawrence, 1877)

Spanish: Esmeralda de Brace

The Brace's Emerald (Riccordia bracei) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). The IUCN Red List lists it as Extinct.
