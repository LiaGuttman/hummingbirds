Hispaniolan Mango

Anthracothorax dominicus (Linnaeus, 1766)

Spanish: Mango Dominicano

The Hispaniolan Mango (Anthracothorax dominicus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 5.8 g and its bill is 27.2 mm long. It lives in Dominican Republic and Haiti. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
