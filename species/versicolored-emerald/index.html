Versicolored Emerald

Chrysuronia versicolor (Vieillot, 1818)

Spanish: Amazilia Versicolor

The Versicolored Emerald (Chrysuronia versicolor) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.1 g and its bill is 19.1 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Paraguay, Venezuela and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
