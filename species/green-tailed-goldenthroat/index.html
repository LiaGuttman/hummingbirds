Green-tailed Goldenthroat

Polytmus theresiae (Silva Maia, 1843)

Spanish: Colibrí de Teresa

The Green-tailed Goldenthroat (Polytmus theresiae) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 3.8 g and its bill is 21.5 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Colombia, Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana, Peru and Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
