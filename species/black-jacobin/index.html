Black Jacobin

Florisuga fusca (Vieillot, 1817)

Spanish: Colibrí Negro

The Black Jacobin (Florisuga fusca) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Florisuginae (topazes). It weighs about 8.0 g and its bill is 25.2 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay and Paraguay. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
