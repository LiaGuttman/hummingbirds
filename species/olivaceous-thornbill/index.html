Olivaceous Thornbill

Chalcostigma olivaceum (Lawrence, 1864)

Spanish: Colibrí Oliváceo

The Olivaceous Thornbill (Chalcostigma olivaceum) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 8.2 g and its bill is 15.3 mm long. It lives in Peru and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
