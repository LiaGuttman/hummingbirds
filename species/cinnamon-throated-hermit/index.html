Cinnamon-throated Hermit

Phaethornis nattereri Berlepsch, 1887

Spanish: Ermitaño de Natterer

The Cinnamon-throated Hermit (Phaethornis nattereri) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Phaethornithinae (hermits). It weighs about 3.1 g and its bill is 26.1 mm long. It lives in Brazil and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
