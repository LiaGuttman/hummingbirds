Anna's Hummingbird

Calypte anna (Lesson, RP, 1829)

Spanish: Colibrí Cabeza Roja

The Anna's Hummingbird (Calypte anna) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Mellisugini (bees). It weighs about 4.2 g and its bill is 20.6 mm long. It lives in United States, Canada and Mexico. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a increasing population.
