Horned Sungem

Heliactin bilophus (Temminck, 1820)

Spanish: Colibrí Cornudito

The Horned Sungem (Heliactin bilophus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 4.4 g and its bill is 18.5 mm long. It lives in Brazil, Bolivia and Suriname. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
