Ruby-topaz Hummingbird

Chrysolampis mosquitus (Linnaeus, 1758)

Spanish: Colibrí Rubí

The Ruby-topaz Hummingbird (Chrysolampis mosquitus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Polytminae (mangoes). It weighs about 3.9 g and its bill is 20.3 mm long. It lives in 13 countries, including Trinidad and Tobago, Colombia, Brazil, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba and Curaçao. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern.
