Marvelous Spatuletail

Loddigesia mirabilis (Bourcier, 1847)

Spanish: Colibrí Admirable

The Marvelous Spatuletail (Loddigesia mirabilis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Heliantheini (brilliants). It weighs about 3.0 g and its bill is 19.3 mm long. It is found only in Peru. Its IUCN Red List status is Near threatened, with a decreasing population.
