Many-spotted Hummingbird

Taphrospilus hypostictus (Gould, 1862)

Spanish: Colibrí Moteado

The Many-spotted Hummingbird (Taphrospilus hypostictus) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 7.0 g and its bill is 25.0 mm long. It lives in Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia and Colombia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
