Green-tailed Emerald

Chlorostilbon alice (Bourcier & Mulsant, 1848)

Spanish: Esmeralda Coliverde

The Green-tailed Emerald (Chlorostilbon alice) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 3.5 g and its bill is 15.2 mm long. It is found only in Venezuela. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
