Talamanca Hummingbird

Eugenes spectabilis (Lawrence, 1867)

Spanish: Colibrí de Talamanca

The Talamanca Hummingbird (Eugenes spectabilis) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Lampornithini (mountain gems). It weighs about 8.0 g and its bill is 36.1 mm long. It lives in Costa Rica and Panama. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a stable population.
