Crowned Woodnymph

Thalurania colombica (Bourcier, 1843)

Spanish: Zafiro Coronado

The Crowned Woodnymph (Thalurania colombica) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Trochilinae (mountain gems, bees and emeralds), tribe Trochilini (emeralds). It weighs about 4.5 g and its bill is 20.4 mm long. It lives in 10 countries, including Costa Rica, Colombia, Ecuador, Panama and Honduras. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
