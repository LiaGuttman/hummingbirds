Rufous-capped Thornbill

Chalcostigma ruficeps (Gould, 1846)

Spanish: Colibrí Capirrufo

The Rufous-capped Thornbill (Chalcostigma ruficeps) is a hummingbird in the subfamily Lesbiinae (coquettes and brilliants), tribe Lesbiini (coquettes). It weighs about 3.4 g and its bill is 15.8 mm long. It lives in Peru, Ecuador and Bolivia. Its IUCN Red List status is Least concern, with a decreasing population.
